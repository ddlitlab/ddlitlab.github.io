text: |
    At the end of its funding period, the DDLitLab can look back on the successful implementation of a pioneering initiative: In the first two one‑year funding rounds, a total of *35 innovative teaching projects* were supported across all faculties of the university. In these projects, digital and hybrid teaching innovations with a strong focus on Data Literacy were developed, whether in *cross‑disciplinary courses*, discipline‑specific modules, or transfer‑oriented formats. The initiative pursued the goal of sustainably integrating *Data Literacy as a key competence* into curricula and creating space for instructors to implement their creative teaching ideas in a methodologically sound way.
 
    With the third funding round, the initiative was further developed for the winter semester 2024/2025 in close collaboration with the <a href="https://www.hcds.uni-hamburg.de/" target="_blank">Hub of Computing & Data Science (HCDS)</a>: the Data Literacy Teaching Lab approach evolved into the *Cross‑Disciplinary Teaching Lab (CDTL)*, within which 10 initiatives were funded. This connection enables even stronger interdisciplinary collaboration and pedagogical‑technological development through integration into the educational ecosystem “Data Literacy Education.” Across all three funding rounds—with 14 Teaching Lab projects in the first round, 21 in the second, and 10 CDTLs in the third—an impressive diversity of *45 innovative teaching initiatives* emerged, anchoring Data Literacy in teaching both within and across disciplines.
